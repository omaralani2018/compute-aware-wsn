Targeted test of CAPS Step 1 (screen) on indoor WSN temperature data
Pre-registered 13 Oct 2026, 13:05 Baghdad, BEFORE the files were downloaded.
Purpose stated openly: the confirmatory sets (PREREG6, PREREG7) have reference suppression below the level at
which the screen can exclude the CNN-LSTM, so the screen was untested there. Datasets are chosen by type
(indoor temperature measured by a wireless sensor network, public, from the data author's repository), not by
any result.

Data (author repositories, github.com/LuisM78):
 A. Appliances energy prediction (Candanedo et al. 2017): energydata_complete.csv, ZigBee WSN in a house,
    10-minute readings. Indoor temperature columns T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T7, T8, T9 (T6 is outside the building and
    is excluded by the dataset's own documentation), evaluated as one deployment with N = 8 nodes (as Intel).
 B. Occupancy detection (Candanedo and Feldheim 2016): datatest.txt, datatraining.txt, datatest2.txt, column
    "Temperature", one office node, 1-minute readings; the three files are joined in time order, duplicate
    timestamps averaged; the series is evaluated as one stream (gaps between files are not bridged or flagged).
Tolerance tau = 0.10 degC (physical, unit 1), as Intel. Everything else as PREREG6 (frozen benchmark, 20 seeds
per learned model, MSE loss, CAPS with B = 96, hmin = 5%).

Reported: the screen's exclusions and, for each, whether the excluded model's median test net saving is <= 0
(correct) or > 0 (false exclusion); if nothing is excluded, the screen is reported as untested again. Also the
headroom flags (validation vs test), the hindsight ceiling and CAPS vs always-CNN-LSTM. No threshold is tuned.
