CASS (Compute-Aware Sink-assisted Switching), per node, two arms {R, L}
- The node runs only the active arm. The sink runs the inactive arm in shadow on the
  shared reconstructed series x~ (x~ = x when sent, forecast when suppressed).
- Sink-side suppression estimate over the trailing W readings:
    active arm : exact (the sink knows which rounds were sent);
    shadow arm : suppressed_hat(t) = |x~(t) - xhat_shadow(t)| / unit <= tau.
  (The sink never sees x on suppressed rounds; this estimator is the information
  limit of shadow evaluation and is reported as such.)
- Switch from a to b at the end of a window iff
    (rho_hat_b - rho_hat_a) * er - (M_b - M_a) * Eop  >  er / W + h * er,
  i.e. the gain must pay the command (amortised over one window) plus a margin h.
  After a switch the node dwells at least W readings. Each switch charges e_cmd = er.
- Start arm: the compute-aware criterion's choice on validation (per seed).
- Tuning: W in {96, 192, 384}, h in {0, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05}; one (W, h) per stream,
  chosen on the VALIDATION span by the median energy over seeds; ties -> larger W,
  then larger h. The test span is evaluated once with the chosen pair.

AMS baseline (Le Borgne et al. 2007, ref [6]), as published
- The node runs all candidate arms (both MACs charged while both are alive), racing
  with the Hoeffding bound, delta = 0.05, model cost C = 1 (relative update rate);
  current model changes only at an update, to the arm with the lowest update rate;
  each change charges e_cmd = er. Secondary: the same AMS with compute not charged,
  to separate the effect of charging computation.

Pairs: R-L1 (lightweight) primary; R-L2 (CNN-LSTM, stored weights) secondary.
Energy per reading as in PREREG.txt (sensing excluded; sink not charged).

Decision rule ("we adopt CASS") requires all of:
 1. CASS < static criterion choice: paired one-sided Wilcoxon over seeds per stream,
    Holm-corrected over the 11 streams at alpha = 0.05, AND median relative saving
    >= 1%, on at least 3 streams.
 2. No stream where CASS's median energy exceeds the static choice by more than 1%.
 3. CASS has lower median energy than AMS on at least 6 of the 11 streams.
 1 and 2 without 3: weaker contribution, decided with the co-author.
 1 not met: reported as a negative result; screening bound kept as the addition.
