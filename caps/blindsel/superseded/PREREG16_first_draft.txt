PREREG16: value of CAPS across radio cost and platform (development streams)
Written 2026-10-15 before any of the quantities below were computed.

Question. How much energy does CAPS save over always running the validation-selected low-cost reference,
and how close does it come to the best static choice in hindsight, when the radio cost per report and the
platform change?

Data (existing, unchanged): 11 development streams at tau = 0.10; per-seed validation and test suppression of
the lightweight model and the CNN-LSTM (stage17/run/learned_*.json; integer-code suppression from
iotlab/fixed/accuracy_q.json for the M3 in integer code); low-cost arms from stage17/run/ms_numbers.json;
per-forecast energies from the platform summaries used by capsplat/caps_platforms.py (run minus idle).

Grid (fixed now, equal to the swept grid of the paper): er in {1e-4, 3.75e-4, 3.8e-4 (anchor), 1e-3, 3e-3} J per report;
platforms {1 nJ per MAC, ATmega128L, M3 software float, M3 integer, nRF52840}.

Method: exactly the computation of capsplat/caps_platforms.py with ER replaced by each grid value
(reference re-selected at each er: by validation suppression at 1 nJ per MAC as in the paper, by validation-span
energy on the platforms); CAPS per seed (Screen then Select), stream energy = median over seeds; totals summed
over the 11 streams; Esense as in frozen_benchmark.

Reported for every grid point, none omitted: CAPS, hindsight static best, always-lightweight and always-CNN-LSTM
totals relative to always-reference (%); CAPS above hindsight (%); CAPS picks.

Status: descriptive, computed after the development results were known; the development streams have been
examined; not a confirmatory test and not used to change CAPS. No threshold or parameter is chosen after seeing
these numbers.
