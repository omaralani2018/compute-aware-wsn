Covariate shift in dual prediction: measurement for the MSE, TA and CLT lightweight models
Pre-registered 13 Oct 2026, 11:30 Baghdad, before any open-loop suppression of these models was computed.
(The closed-loop test suppressions of all three are already known: PREREG3, PREREG5.)

Definition. On the test span, open-loop suppression rho_open = share of steps whose forecast from the TRUE
last 12 readings is within tau of the reading (the forecast a model would make with no reconstruction);
closed-loop suppression rho_closed = the stored test suppression on the shared reconstructed state.
Shift gap g = rho_open - rho_closed: the suppression lost because deployment inputs differ from training
inputs (covariate shift induced by the send-on-delta reconstruction). 11 streams, 20 seeds, three models.

Hypotheses:
 H1 CLT has a smaller gap than MSE: paired one-sided Wilcoxon over seeds, Holm across 11 streams, alpha 0.05;
    supported if significant on at least 6 of 11 streams and significantly larger on none.
 H2 Across the 11 streams, the median gap reduction (MSE - CLT) and the median closed-loop gain (CLT - MSE)
    have a positive Spearman correlation (reported with its value; supported if rho_s > 0.5).
Reported for TA as well. Descriptive; no threshold is tuned.
