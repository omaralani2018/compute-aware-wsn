Batched reports on the nodes' own radios (FIT IoT-LAB). PREREG16.
Written 16 Nov 2026, before this firmware was flashed and before any measurement of it.
Purpose: test, by measurement, the batching analysis of RULE5 (Paper 1, direction A): the linear frame-cost model and
the energy per reading of immediate dual prediction (S0), raw batching (S1, 25 readings per frame) and batched dual
prediction (S2, 5 B per report, frame at 20 reports or after 25 readings).
Nodes and profiles as PREREG15: M3 grenoble TX m3-21 / RX m3-22 (profile caps_m3); nRF52840DK saclay TX 5 / RX 6
(profile caps_custom). Another node of the same type and site may replace an unavailable one; it is recorded.
Firmware: RIOT 2025.07 (commit eb65305c), arm-none-eabi-gcc, app exp16/app (main.c, Makefile, replay_tab.h generated by
gen_sched.py), GNRC link layer only, channel 22, PAN 0x0023, broadcast, default power and CSMA-CA. Builds ROLE=TX/RX.
TX sequence: 60 s wait; SYNC as PREREG15; then 5 rounds of: IDLE; SWEEP (payload 4, 20, 40, 60, 80, 100 B, 4 s of
back-to-back broadcast frames each, IDLE after each); REPLAY of 9 cells and a NULL cell (IDLE after each).
REPLAY: one reading every 20 ms; at the readings listed in the schedule the radio is woken, one frame of the listed
size is sent and the radio is put to sleep; the CPU sleeps between readings. The NULL cell has 1,500 readings and no frame.
Schedules (gen_sched.py): validation-selected reference of the paper on the test span, first 1,500 readings (all 339 of
traffic): taxi, traffic, Intel mote column 0. Predicted energy per reading above idle, from the PREREG15 points:

Analysis (analyse_batch.py, checked on synthetic traces before any run; recovered energies within 5%):
 alignment as PREREG15 (sweep and SYNC on, IDLE off, replay masked), accepted if correlation >= 0.8;
 sweep: E per frame = (P - P_idle) x wall / frames, median over rounds;
 replay: E per reading = (P - P_idle) x wall / readings minus the NULL cell of the same round, median over rounds.
 Validity: no send error and RX count >= 99% of frames per phase; invalid phases reported and not used.
Predictions and outcomes (each reported whatever its value):
 P1 the frame energy is linear in payload: at 20, 40, 60, 80 B within 10% of the line through this run's 4 and 100 B.
 P2 per stream, the measured order of S0, S1, S2 equals the predicted order (taxi S2<S1<S0; traffic S1<S2<S0;
    Intel S2<S0<S1), on each node type.
 P3 number of the 9 cells whose measured energy per reading is within 15% of the prediction from this run's sweep line.
 Also reported: the 4 B and 100 B frame energies against PREREG15, late wake-ups, the NULL energy per reading.
Runs: one experiment per site; a technical failure (no capture, node failure, flashing error) may be repeated once with
the same firmware, with the reason recorded. Nothing is rerun to change a result.
Limits stated now: sender side only, broadcast without acknowledgement, no MAC duty-cycling, no frame loss model,
replay compressed in time (20 ms per reading), so the sleep power between readings is not that of the real interval.
