PREREG16 (batched reports) commands. Run from the computer that holds your IoT-LAB SSH key (LOGIN = bakr).
Files needed in the current folder: b16_tx_m3.elf, b16_rx_m3.elf, b16_tx_nrf.elf, b16_rx_nrf.elf.

=== A. M3, grenoble (about 30 minutes) ===
1) scp b16_tx_m3.elf b16_rx_m3.elf bakr@grenoble.iot-lab.info:~/
2) ssh bakr@grenoble.iot-lab.info
3) iotlab-experiment submit -n b16_m3 -d 40 -l grenoble,m3,21,b16_tx_m3.elf,caps_m3 -l grenoble,m3,22,b16_rx_m3.elf
   iotlab-experiment wait
4) iotlab-node --stop -l grenoble,m3,21+22
   iotlab-node --start -l grenoble,m3,21+22
5) Right away (the sender waits 60 s): serial_aggregator | tee b16_m3_serial.txt
6) Wait for "ALL DONE" (about 26 minutes after step 4), wait 30 s more, then Ctrl+C.
7) iotlab-experiment stop
   ls -la ~/.iot-lab/EXPID/consumption/
8) On your computer:
   scp bakr@grenoble.iot-lab.info:~/b16_m3_serial.txt .
   scp bakr@grenoble.iot-lab.info:~/.iot-lab/EXPID/consumption/m3_21.oml b16_m3_21.oml

=== B. nRF52840, saclay (about 30 minutes) ===
1) scp b16_tx_nrf.elf b16_rx_nrf.elf bakr@saclay.iot-lab.info:~/
2) ssh bakr@saclay.iot-lab.info
3) iotlab-experiment submit -n b16_nrf -d 40 -l saclay,nrf52840dk,5,b16_tx_nrf.elf,caps_custom -l saclay,nrf52840dk,6,b16_rx_nrf.elf
   iotlab-experiment wait
4) iotlab-node --stop -l saclay,nrf52840dk,5+6
   iotlab-node --start -l saclay,nrf52840dk,5+6
5) serial_aggregator | tee b16_nrf_serial.txt
6) Wait for "ALL DONE" (about 26 minutes after step 4), 30 s more, Ctrl+C.
7) iotlab-experiment stop ; ls -la ~/.iot-lab/EXPID/consumption/
8) scp bakr@saclay.iot-lab.info:~/b16_nrf_serial.txt .
   scp bakr@saclay.iot-lab.info:~/.iot-lab/EXPID/consumption/nrf52840dk_5.oml b16_nrf_5.oml

Send the four files (two serial captures, two .oml files) with the two experiment ids.
If a node is busy, use another node of the same type for that role and write down which one.
If the capture shows "ERROR no network interface", repeat steps 4 to 6 once.
