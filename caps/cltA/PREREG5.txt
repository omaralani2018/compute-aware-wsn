Closed-loop tolerance-aware training (CLT) of the lightweight predictor: pilot
Pre-registered 13 Oct 2026, 09:00 Baghdad, BEFORE any CLT model was trained.
This is the third model-improvement test after CASS (stopped) and TA (rule not met);
all three are reported whatever the outcome.

Hypothesis: learned predictors lose partly because they are trained on true histories
but forecast from reconstructed ones. CLT trains on the inputs the deployed node sees.

Procedure (per stream and seed, seeds 0-19, 11 streams, tau = 0.10):
- Round 0 = the TA model already trained (PREREG3), with its selected margin c.
- Rounds 1-3: run closed-loop dual prediction over the TRAINING span with the current
  model; build 4,000 windows whose inputs come from the reconstructed series and whose
  targets are the true readings; add them to the training set (aggregation over rounds,
  plus the original true-history windows); fine-tune 50 epochs with the TA loss (same c);
  evaluate closed-loop validation suppression after 25 and 50 epochs.
- Select, on VALIDATION suppression only, among round 0 and the 6 checkpoints of rounds 1-3
  (ties: earlier). Test span evaluated once. Architecture, normalisation, data unchanged.

Decision rule (same bar as PREREG3, applied to CLT against the original MSE models):
 1. CLT test rho > MSE test rho significantly (one-sided Wilcoxon over seeds, Holm across
    11 streams, alpha 0.05) on at least 4 of 11, and significantly lower on none.
 2. Streams where the learned model's net energy saving over the reference is significant
    (one-sided Wilcoxon, Holm) rise from 3 (MSE) to at least 5.
 GO only if both hold. Also reported: CLT vs TA, paired. Nothing tuned on the test span.
