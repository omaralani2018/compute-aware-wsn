PREREG17: dual prediction run end to end on IoT-LAB nodes over their own IEEE 802.15.4 radios (NETX)
Written 15 Oct 2026, before any run. Frozen by the SHA-256 of this file and of the files listed in MANIFEST.sha256.

Purpose. Sections 4.4 and 5.1 measure the cost of a forecast and the cost of a report separately and combine them with
the closed-loop suppression computed on the host. Here dual prediction runs on the nodes: each round a sender node
forecasts and transmits a real frame to a sink node only when the forecast misses by more than the tolerance. The test is
whether the energy the node spends ranks the predictors as the separate measurements predict, and whether the choice CAPS
makes before deployment is the one that costs least on the node.

Nodes and radio. As PREREG15 and AMENDMENT_15a: IoT-LAB M3 pair (grenoble, sender m3-21, sink m3-22) and nRF52840DK pair
(saclay, sender 5, sink 6); if a node is unavailable, another node of the same type is used and recorded. Same channel,
PAN, addresses and driver defaults as PREREG15. Each report is one 7-byte frame (stream and arm, round index, the reading
as 4 bytes), UNICAST to the sink WITH acknowledgement. Between reports the sender's radio sleeps; the sink listens
throughout (its energy is not measured) and prints every frame it receives. Consumption profiles as PREREG12 and 15.
Firmware: RIOT commit eb65305c, arm-none-eabi-gcc 13.2.1, -Os, -std=c11 (no floating-point contraction).
Builds: m3_float (M3, software float), m3_int (M3, PREREG13 integer predictors and integer readings), nrf (nRF52840, FPU).

Streams and predictors. Four development streams: taxi, exchange_cpc, machine_temp, cpu; tau = 0.10 in the normalised
units of the paper. The node stores the first R = 480 rounds of the test span plus the 12 preceding readings (480 because
exchange_cpc has 488 test rounds). Arms per stream: the reference that CAPS uses (Section 5.1 rule at er = 3.8e-4 J:
linear extrapolation K = 2 for taxi, exponential smoothing for the other three; the same on the three builds), the
lightweight model (seed 0) and the CNN-LSTM (lowest stored seed: taxi s5, exchange_cpc s19, machine_temp s0, cpu s0).
Weights and normalisation exactly as in the paper; integer build as PREREG13 (qemu.py scheme), with the transmit rule
|x - forecast| > floor(tau x unit x 2^fi) on Q(fi) integers.

Round. Each round: forecast from the shared state; if the forecast misses, send the report and store the reading,
otherwise store the forecast; then sleep (CPU idle, radio asleep) to the next period boundary. Period: 20 ms for the
reference and the lightweight model on all builds; for the CNN-LSTM 400 ms (m3_float), 120 ms (m3_int), 60 ms (nrf), each
above its slowest measured forecast plus one report. The energy per round above idle does not depend on the period; the
short period for the fast arms concentrates their energy against the noise of the power monitor.

Sender sequence (nothing printed inside a phase): 60-s wait (AMENDMENT_15a), IDLE 5 s, SYNC 12 s (radio in receive mode),
IDLE 5 s; then PASSES passes (3 on the M3 builds, 5 on nrf), each running for every stream the three arms in the order
reference, lightweight, CNN-LSTM, each RUN of R rounds followed by IDLE 5 s; then SYNC 12 s and IDLE 5 s. After each RUN
the sender prints transmissions, send errors, late rounds, forecast and active cycles, wall time, and the transmit
decisions as a bitmap. Expected duration: m3_float 47 min, m3_int 20 min, nrf 23 min.

Host check (done before freezing, passed). The firmware's own predictor and transmit-rule code (app/netx_pred.h),
compiled with the firmware's compiler, flags and newlib and run under qemu-arm, gives on all 480 rounds the same transmit
decisions as the Python emulation (gen.py float64; gen2.py bit-exact integer): 52 of 52 stream-arm-build combinations
(check/check.py, check/out_*.txt). The expected bitmaps are in check/expected_decisions.json.

Measurements (analyse_netx.py, tested on synthetic traces from sim_netx.py before any run).
 Alignment: the clock offset (search -120 to 120 s, step 0.01 s) maximising the correlation between the 45-sample running
 median of power and the pattern SYNC on, neighbouring IDLE off, over the two SYNC blocks. Accepted if that correlation is
 at least 0.9 and the offsets found from the start and the end SYNC separately agree within 0.1 s; otherwise the build is
 reported as descriptive only. (On the PREREG15 traces this rule gives correlation 0.999 and 0.997.)
 Energy per round above idle: E_meas = (P_run - P_idle) x wall time / R, with P_run the mean power of the RUN phase and
 P_idle the mean of the two IDLE phases on either side of it, each trimmed by 0.3 s at both ends. Per arm: the median over
 the valid passes. Gross energy per round is reported as well.
 Prediction from the separate measurements (fixed now): E_pred = E_forecast + n_tx/R x E_report_UC, with E_forecast the
 Section 5.1 above-idle energy per forecast of that arm on that build, E_report_UC the PREREG15 unicast report energy of
 that node type (4-byte payload; the 3 extra bytes here add about 0.1 ms of airtime), and n_tx the node's own
 transmissions. The predicted values are listed in netx_plan.json. E_pred leaves out the fixed cost of each round (timer
 wake-up, the transmit test, bookkeeping), estimated at 1% to 2% on the M3 and up to about 5% for the least costly nRF
 arm; it is the same for the three arms of a cell.

Validity. A RUN is valid if it had no send error, the sink received at least 99% of the frames the sender sent in it, and
its power phases lie inside the trace. An arm has a measured value if at least half of the passes the protocol runs (and
at least two) are valid. A cell (build x stream) enters
O1 to O3 if alignment was accepted and its three arms have measured values; excluded cells are counted.

Outcomes (fixed now; counts over the valid cells of the 12 build-stream cells).
 O1 Ranking: the arm with the lowest E_meas has E_pred within 5% of the lowest E_pred of the cell (predicted differences
    under 5% are ties, and either order counts as agreement).
 O2 Accuracy: E_meas within +-15% of E_pred, per arm and cell.
 O3 CAPS on the node: the arm CAPS selects before deployment, with the node's own report energy and validation
    suppression (seed 0 lightweight, the stored CNN-LSTM seed; listed in netx_plan.json "choice"), has E_meas within 5% of
    the lowest E_meas of the cell.
 O4 Loop: the node's transmit decisions equal the expected bitmaps in every pass; the readings received by the sink equal
    the sender's readings bit for bit. With both, the sink's reconstruction equals the sender's state, within tau on
    every round.
 Reported in addition (descriptive): compute-blind selection's E_meas against CAPS's, summed over the valid cells of a
 build; late rounds; cycles; gross energy per round.

Runs. One run per build. A technical failure (node unavailable, crash, capture missed, radio not initialised) is rerun
with the same firmware and recorded. A completed valid run is reported whatever it shows; nothing is rerun to change a
result. Any deviation from this text is written as an amendment before the affected data are analysed.
