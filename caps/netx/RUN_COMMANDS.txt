PREREG17 (NETX) commands. Run from the computer that holds your IoT-LAB SSH key. Replace LOGIN with your user name.
Files needed in the current folder (from the fw folder): netx_tx_m3_float.elf, netx_tx_m3_int.elf, netx_rx_m3.elf,
netx_tx_nrf.elf, netx_rx_nrf.elf.

=== A. M3 float, grenoble (about 50 minutes) ===
1) scp netx_tx_m3_float.elf netx_tx_m3_int.elf netx_rx_m3.elf LOGIN@grenoble.iot-lab.info:~/
2) ssh LOGIN@grenoble.iot-lab.info
3) iotlab-experiment submit -n netx_m3f -d 60 -l grenoble,m3,21,netx_tx_m3_float.elf,caps_m3 -l grenoble,m3,22,netx_rx_m3.elf
   iotlab-experiment wait
4) Power-cycle both nodes (AMENDMENT_15a):
   iotlab-node --stop -l grenoble,m3,21+22
   iotlab-node --start -l grenoble,m3,21+22
5) Right away (the sender waits 60 s), start the capture in the foreground:
   serial_aggregator | tee netx_m3f_serial.txt
6) Wait for "ALL DONE" (about 48 minutes after step 4), wait 30 s more, then Ctrl+C.
7) iotlab-experiment stop
   ls -la ~/.iot-lab/EXPID/consumption/
8) Back on your computer:
   scp LOGIN@grenoble.iot-lab.info:~/netx_m3f_serial.txt .
   scp LOGIN@grenoble.iot-lab.info:~/.iot-lab/EXPID/consumption/m3_21.oml netx_m3f_21.oml

=== B. M3 integer, grenoble (about 25 minutes) ===
Same as A with: -n netx_m3i -d 30 and netx_tx_m3_int.elf in place of netx_tx_m3_float.elf;
capture file netx_m3i_serial.txt; "ALL DONE" about 21 minutes after step 4; copy m3_21.oml as netx_m3i_21.oml.

=== C. nRF52840, saclay (about 30 minutes) ===
1) scp netx_tx_nrf.elf netx_rx_nrf.elf LOGIN@saclay.iot-lab.info:~/
2) ssh LOGIN@saclay.iot-lab.info
3) iotlab-experiment submit -n netx_nrf -d 35 -l saclay,nrf52840dk,5,netx_tx_nrf.elf,caps_custom -l saclay,nrf52840dk,6,netx_rx_nrf.elf
   iotlab-experiment wait
4) iotlab-node --stop -l saclay,nrf52840dk,5+6
   iotlab-node --start -l saclay,nrf52840dk,5+6
5) serial_aggregator | tee netx_nrf_serial.txt
6) Wait for "ALL DONE" (about 24 minutes after step 4), 30 s more, Ctrl+C.
7) iotlab-experiment stop ; ls -la ~/.iot-lab/EXPID/consumption/
8) scp LOGIN@saclay.iot-lab.info:~/netx_nrf_serial.txt .
   scp LOGIN@saclay.iot-lab.info:~/.iot-lab/EXPID/consumption/nrf52840dk_5.oml netx_nrf_5.oml

Send the six files (three serial captures, three .oml files) with the three experiment ids.
If a node is busy, use another node of the same type for both roles' place and write down which ones.
If the capture shows "ERROR no network interface", stop, and repeat steps 4 to 6 once.
