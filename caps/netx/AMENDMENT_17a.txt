Amendment 17a to PREREG17, written 15 Oct 2026 after the first M3 float run and before the M3 integer and nRF52840 runs.
First M3 float run: experiment 455253 (grenoble, sender m3-21, sink m3-22), analysed with the frozen analyse_netx.py.
The run was technically complete (36 of 36 RUN phases valid, decisions and received readings equal to the expected ones),
but it failed the PREREG17 alignment rule: the offsets fitted to the start and the end SYNC blocks were 36.95 s and 37.07 s,
a difference of 0.12 s against the 0.1-s tolerance (joint correlation 0.989). The difference is clock drift between the
serial log and the power monitor over the 45-min run. Under PREREG17 its O1 to O3 are descriptive; it is reported as such,
with its numbers, next to the confirmatory runs. Its files are kept (data/netx_m3f_serial.txt, data/netx_m3f_21.oml).
Change (analysis only; firmware, sequence, energy definition, predictions, validity and outcomes unchanged):
 1. Alignment: each SYNC block is fitted separately (same search and pattern as PREREG17); the offset of any time in the
    serial log is the linear interpolation between the two fits. Accepted if both fits reach correlation 0.9 and their
    difference (the drift over the run) is at most 0.5 s. Implemented in analyse_netx_17a.py, tested on synthetic traces
    with 0, 0.3 and 0.8 s of drift (accepted, accepted, rejected).
 2. M3 float is run once more with the same firmware; that second run is the confirmatory M3 float run. The M3 integer and
    nRF52840 runs are confirmatory under this amendment. Each confirmatory run is reported whatever it shows.
 3. sim_netx.py (test tool only) gained the drift option SIM_DRIFT used for these tests; its new SHA-256 is in AMENDMENT_17a.sha256.
