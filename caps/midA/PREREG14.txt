Two further analyses, pre-registered 13 Oct 2026 before any code for them was run.

A. Model size versus architecture (the 208-MAC network and the 99,488-MAC CNN-LSTM differ in both, so size and
   architecture cannot be separated with these two models alone).
   Two mid-size learned predictors are added, everything else as the frozen benchmark (12-reading look-back, MSE loss,
   20 seeds, epoch cap 200, checkpoints every 25, validation-selected checkpoint, receiver verification, 11 streams):
     mlp_mid       : Dense 64 ReLU, Dense 32 ReLU, Dense 1           M = 12*64 + 64*32 + 32         = 2,848 MACs
     cnnlstm_small : Conv1D 8 (kernel 3, same) ReLU, LSTM 8, Dense 1 M = 12*3*8 + 12*4*8*(8+8) + 8 = 6,440 MACs
   (MAC counting as in Section 5.1 for the 99,488-MAC CNN-LSTM.)
   Tests (two-sided Mann-Whitney U on test suppression over the 20 seeds of each model, Holm across the 11 streams):
     (i)  architecture at similar size: mlp_mid vs cnnlstm_small;
     (ii) size within architecture: mlp_mid vs lightweight (208), cnnlstm_small vs cnn_lstm (99,488).
   Energy verdict of each new model against the validation-selected reference at er = 3.8e-4 J and 1 nJ per MAC, as in
   Section 6.3 (one-sided Wilcoxon over seeds in each direction, Holm across streams), and the CAPS screen with the new
   models as candidates (false exclusions counted as in Section 6.11).
   Conclusion rule fixed now:
     "size, not architecture" if (i) is significant on at most 2 of 11 streams and (ii) on at least 6 of 11 for at least
     one architecture; "architecture" if (i) is significant on at least 6 of 11 in one direction; otherwise "mixed".
     The selection conclusions of the paper stand if neither new model saves energy significantly on more streams than
     the lightweight model (3 of 11) and the screen makes no false exclusion.
   Secondary (labelled estimate): net saving with the measured nJ per MAC of Section 6.14 for the same architecture
   family (MLP: lightweight value; CNN-LSTM: CNN-LSTM value) on each platform.

B. Predictability statistic tested on datasets not used in development.
   The training-span statistic of Section 6.6 (fraction of first differences within tau on the training span; within
   segments for SensorScope) and closed-loop persistence test suppression at tau = 0.10 are computed on the 13 datasets
   not used in development (Grand St Bernard, six NAB, four SensorScope, two indoor). Fastness (Eq. 7) is reported
   alongside.
   Confirmed if Spearman(statistic, persistence suppression) >= 0.6 with one-sided p < 0.05 and mean absolute error
   <= 0.10 on the 13; also reported pooled with the 11 development streams (24). Descriptive: Spearman between the
   statistic and the lightweight model's median suppression difference from the reference over the 24 datasets.
No threshold is tuned and nothing is rerun to change a result.
